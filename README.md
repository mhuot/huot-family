# Louis Huot's Movements

A one-page history of where Louis Huot (1827 to 1893) lived, year by year: from L'Ange-Gardien on the Beaupré coast of Quebec, by way of Detroit in 1849, to St. Anthony, Medicine Lake and Corcoran in Minnesota, and in 1876 to the Old Crossing of the Red Lake River, the place now called Huot. It has a timeline drawn to scale, a map, and the records behind each date, with the French ones put into English.

Read it at https://mhuot.github.io/huot-family/
